# Project 01 — Retail Sales Performance

## Business Problem
NovaMart leadership needs a dashboard to analyze sales across cities and product categories. They cannot see which categories are actually profitable after discounts.

## Questions the report must answer
1. What is the total Revenue and Gross Margin by Category?
2. Which City generates the highest Average Order Value (AOV)?
3. How do sales trend month-over-month?
4. Which products have the highest discount rates?
5. How do New vs. Returning customers differ in spending?

## KPI Definitions
- **Total Revenue**: Sum of the final Revenue column.
- **Total Cost**: Sum of (Quantity * Unit_Cost).
- **Gross Margin**: Total Revenue - Total Cost.
- **Margin %**: Gross Margin / Total Revenue.
- **AOV**: Total Revenue / Distinct Order Count.

## Success Criteria
- Interactive report with Slicers (City, Category).
- KPI cards for Revenue, Margin %, AOV.
- Data is fully cleaned in Power Query (handling duplicates, bad dates, text inconsistencies).

## Out of Scope
- Forecasting
- Row-level security
